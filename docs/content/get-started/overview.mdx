GitQuire is an IDE for co-creating markdown-based knowledge bases and documents with the agents you already use.

<AgentIcons />

GitQuire runs as a web app on your own server, from one [Docker image](../gitquire/run-with-docker.mdx). Your team signs in with GitHub and edits in the browser, also on phones and tablets, and every page is a Markdown file in your GitHub repository.

## Three layers

GitQuire is three layers working together: a WYSIWYG editor for you, MCP and skills for agents, and your markdown files underneath.

<LayerStack />

## Additional Features
- **[Skills Studio](../features/skills/index.mdx)**: write reusable instructions for your AI tools, or find a skill in the catalog.
- **[AI chat](../gitquire/ai-chat.mdx)**: chat with Claude or Mistral about the wiki without leaving GitQuire, or connect an external agent such as Claude to it.
- Discuss passages in **[comment threads](../gitquire/comments.mdx)** stored in the page, and let your AI agent pick them up.
- **[Auto-sync](../features/git/sync.mdx)** knowledge base changes with your team using Git.
- Explore your knowledge base through **[graph and wiki-link visualizations](../reference/core-concepts.md#links-and-backlinks)**.
- [Connect to GitQuire](../remote-control/connecting-agents.mdx) remotely from local agents, remote agents, or other devices.
