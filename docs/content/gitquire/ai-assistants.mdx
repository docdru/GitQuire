title: Connect Claude and Mistral
icon: LuBot
description: Let Claude or Mistral's Le Chat read and edit the wiki as you, from the web, desktop and phone.
---

Claude and Mistral's Le Chat can work in GitQuire as you: their edits appear live for everyone and
are committed with you as the author. Each person connects their own account; nothing runs on the
server on their behalf.

This needs [GitHub sign-in](./github-sign-in.mdx), a public HTTPS address and
`GITQUIRE_MCP_CONNECTORS=on`, because the assistants connect from their providers' servers. In the
app, **Settings → Connect Claude and Mistral** shows the address to use and the steps below.

Your connector address is your wiki's address with `/mcp` at the end, for example
`https://wiki.example.com/mcp`.

## Claude

1. In Claude, open **Customize → Connectors**, click **+** and choose **Add custom connector**.
2. Name it GitQuire, paste the connector address and click **Add**.
3. Click **Connect**, sign in with GitHub and approve access.

On a Team or Enterprise plan, an owner adds the connector once under **Organization settings →
Connectors**, then everyone clicks **Connect**. For Claude Code, create a personal token on
`/auth/agents` instead.

## Mistral Le Chat

1. In [Le Chat](https://chat.mistral.ai/), open **Intelligence → Connectors** and click
   **+ Add Connector**.
2. Switch to **Custom MCP Connector**, name it `gitquire` (no spaces) and paste the connector
   address.
3. Click **Connect**, sign in with GitHub and approve access.

Only Le Chat administrators can add connectors; on the Free, Pro and Student plans, that is the
account owner. Turn the connector on in a chat to let Le Chat use the wiki there.

## Managing access

Every connected app appears on `/auth/agents`, where you can revoke it. An access token lasts an
hour and is renewed for up to 90 days of inactivity. Who gets in is still decided by GitHub sign-in
and the repository's access rules; see [Security](./security.mdx).
